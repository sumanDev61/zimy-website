Zimy – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Summary

Zimy does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

What is stored on your phone

Your nickname, optional bio, optional profile photo, XP and level, equipped frame and intro, daily check-in streak, game statistics and your sound setting. All of this stays in the app's private storage on your device.

Profile photo

If you choose a photo, Android's photo picker gives Zimy only that one image. Zimy saves a copy in its private storage so it can show your avatar. It is never uploaded.

Backups

If you have Android's device backup turned on, Android may include Zimy's data in your personal Google backup. This is controlled by your device settings, not by Zimy.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete all data in the app, or uninstall the app. Either one removes everything Zimy stored.

Children

Zimy does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be included in an app update and on this page.
